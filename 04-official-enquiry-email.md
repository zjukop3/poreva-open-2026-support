## The email

Dear Poreva Open Organising Team,

We are preparing a submission for the first three challenges (EGFR, TNF-α, GLP1R) and would
like to confirm a small number of points before we finalise. We have read the Challenge
pages, the FAQ and the Official Competition Terms; the questions below are the ones we could
not resolve from those documents.

**1. Team eligibility and "work email verifies affiliation".**
Teams must have at least three members and affiliation is verified through a work email.
(a) Must the three members come from three *different* organisations, or may they share one?
(b) What exactly does the verification check — the email domain, a matching institutional
page, or something further?
(c) How should an independent researcher without an institutional address be handled? Is a
personal or self-hosted domain ever acceptable, with or without supporting documentation?

**2. Competition roles and AI-agent assistance.**
Is it compliant if one member operates an AI agent that performs the design work, while the
other two members contribute in other capacities (evaluation, structural analysis, writing)?
We want to be sure that a small team using agentic tooling satisfies the "meaningful
contribution by each member" expectation rather than only the headcount requirement.

**3. Resubmission / overwriting.**
May a team revise or replace its submission for a challenge after submitting, or is the
first submission final? The FAQ notes that wet-lab capacity is allocated on a first-come
basis, which suggests an early submission is advantageous — but we would like to know whether
an early submission forecloses a later corrected one.

**4. Scoring threshold on human affinity.**
The scoring table refers to human affinity "above threshold". Could you state the actual
threshold, or confirm the value is intentionally withheld until after the wet-lab phase?

**5. Licensing of released designs.**
Could you point to the specific clause in the Official Competition Terms that governs
publication of submitted designs, and confirm whether design release occurs for all
submissions or only for winning entries? This affects whether a participant with a possible
future patent filing should submit at all.

**6. Payment arrangements.**
For team members who are not Chinese tax residents, how is the prize money paid? Is the
RMB amount converted at award time, and are there any account-residency requirements?

**7. Application review turnaround.**
Is the "apply to compete" step reviewed manually, and what is a typical turnaround time? We
would like to understand how late a team can complete registration and still be eligible for
challenges that open later.

**8. Wet-lab scope for challenge winners.**
When will the size and design of the wet-lab validation for each challenge winner be
announced (assay format, number of designs tested, whether affinity and selectivity are both
measured)?

If it is useful, we are happy to share a short description of our design and evaluation
pipeline. We would also appreciate being told of any additional requirement we have not
anticipated.

Thank you for organising the competition.

## Notes on the draft

- **Ordering is deliberate.** Eligibility questions come first because they are the only
  ones that can block submission outright; payment and licensing come later.
- **Q2 is phrased as a compliance question, not a boast.** Disclosing AI-agent use up front is
  safer than having it discovered in review, and the answer determines how you describe the
  team's division of labour on the submission form.
- **Q3 is the one with timing consequences.** If resubmission is allowed, an early submission
  is strictly better; if not, the first version must be final. This changes the submission
  schedule.
- **Do not send duplicate questions** you have already had answered in writing; trim this list
  to what is still open.
- Keep the email to **one screen and one thread**: a follow-up is easier if the original
  message is short and numbered.
